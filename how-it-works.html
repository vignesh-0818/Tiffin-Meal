How It Works

Your Journey to Fresh Meals

Follow these simple steps to start enjoying delicious home-cooked food delivered to your door

1

Choose a Plan

Browse our vegetarian, non-vegetarian, diet, and combo plans. Pick one that fits your lifestyle and budget.

2

Select Your Cuisine

Choose from North Indian, South Indian, Gujarati, Kerala, and more. Mix and match cuisines each day.

3

Select Dietary Preference

Tell us about your dietary needs - vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, or low-calorie. We accommodate all preferences.

4

Customize Your Meals

Pick your spice level, portion size, and specific dishes for each day of the week.

5

Schedule Delivery

Set your preferred delivery time slot. We deliver Monday through Saturday between 11:30 AM and 1:00 PM.

6

Receive Fresh Meals

Enjoy hot, fresh, home-cooked meals delivered right to your doorstep in eco-friendly packaging.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about getting started with TiffinBox

Once you complete your registration and select your plan, we can start delivering as early as the next day. Our kitchen team needs just a few hours to prepare your customized meal plan. For next-day delivery, sign up before 4:00 PM.

Absolutely! You can modify your plan, cuisine preferences, spice level, and portion sizes anytime from your dashboard. Need to skip a day? Just let us know at least 6 hours before your scheduled delivery and we'll pause that day's meal with a credit applied to your account.

We take allergies and dietary restrictions very seriously. During registration, you can specify any allergies (nuts, dairy, gluten, soy, etc.) and our kitchen team will ensure your meals are completely free from those ingredients. We also offer dedicated Jain, vegan, keto, and low-sodium plans.

All meals are prepared fresh on the day of delivery and packed in insulated, eco-friendly containers that maintain temperature for up to 2 hours. Our delivery partners follow optimized routes to ensure your tiffin arrives within 30 minutes of leaving our kitchen.

We currently deliver across Delhi NCR, including areas in New Delhi, Gurgaon, Noida, Faridabad, and Ghaziabad. We're constantly expanding our delivery zones. Enter your pincode during signup to check if we service your area.
