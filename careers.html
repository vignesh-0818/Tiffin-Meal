More Than a Job

Why Work at SparklePro

We've built a company our people love — with fair pay, real training and a culture that celebrates great work.

Competitive hourly rates, weekly pay, paid travel time and tips passed straight to you. No wage games, ever.

Paid Training

Two weeks of hands-on training with a mentor, plus certifications in specialised services as you grow.

Career Growth

Clear paths from cleaner to team supervisor to operations — most of our managers started on the tools.

Flexible Shifts

Morning, afternoon or weekend shifts that fit around study, family or a second job. You choose your schedule.
