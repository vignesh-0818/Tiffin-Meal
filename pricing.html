Yes, you can pause your subscription for up to 30 days from your dashboard. During the pause period, no meals will be delivered and no charges will apply. Simply resume when you are ready, and your plan will continue from where you left off.

If you are unsatisfied with a meal, please contact us within 2 hours of delivery. We will either replace the meal or issue a full refund for that day. For subscription cancellations, unused days in your current billing cycle are refunded in full.
