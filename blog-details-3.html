Kitchen Cleaning Checklist for Every Home

Ava Williams
Kitchen Cleaning Specialist, SparklePro
May 22, 2026 5 min read Checklists

The kitchen is where every family day starts and ends — which also makes it the fastest room in the house to look tired. The secret is not cleaning more often; it is cleaning in the right order. This checklist mirrors the system our SparklePro crews use, scaled to fit any kitchen, from a compact apartment to a family-sized workspace.

1. Clear and Organise First

You cannot clean what is still on the worktop. Start by moving everything off the counters — appliances, toasters, spice racks and that slowly growing "misc" pile. Wipe each item down before it goes back, and give everything you return a permanent home. A clear worktop is the single fastest way to make a kitchen look freshly cleaned.

2. Daily Essentials That Keep Momentum

A few two-minute habits between deep cleans keep grease and grime from ever taking hold:

  • Wipe the hob and splashback immediately after cooking while heat makes grease easier to shift.
  • Empty and rinse the sink after every meal so food never dries on.
  • Run the bin out nightly in warmer months to prevent odours and fruit flies.
  • Keep a spray bottle and microfibre cloth within arm's reach of the worktop.

3. The Weekly Surface Checklist

Once a week, run the full surface circuit. Work top to bottom and back to front, and tick each task off as you go:

  • Hob, rings and control knobs — degreased and wiped dry.
  • Splashback and extractor fan — lifted off with hot soapy water.
  • Worktops and counter edges — sprayed and wiped in one direction.
  • Cabinet fronts and handles — wiped down, including the tops of high cabinets.
  • Microwave interior — steamed clean with a damp cloth and dried.
  • Fridge door seals and exterior — wiped and polished.
  • Window sill and sill tracks — dusted and wiped.
  • Floor — swept, then mopped with a suitable cleaner.
If a splashback is already greasy, apply the degreaser, let it sit for three minutes, then wipe. Patience does half the work for you.

4. Monthly Appliances and Drawers Deep-Dive

Once a month, go beyond the surfaces. Empty one drawer or cabinet at a time, wipe the inside, and return only what you actually use. Pull out the refrigerator to vacuum the vent and coil dust — a dusty coil makes the appliance work harder and cost more. Descale the kettle, run an empty cycle through the dishwasher with a cleaner and soak the extractor filters overnight in degreaser.

This is also the perfect moment to check for hidden spills: the jam jar that slid behind the cereal, the crumbs in the cutlery drawer. These tiny details are exactly what professional clients notice first when a kitchen truly shines.

5. Hygiene Beyond the Worktop

A clean kitchen does not stop at the door frame. Door handles, light switches and the bin lid are touched dozens of times a day and are among the most germ-dense surfaces in any home. Give them a quick wipe with a kitchen-safe disinfectant every week.

Finally, never re-use the same cloth across the kitchen and bathroom. Colour-coded cloths — one for food areas, one for general touchpoints — stop cross-contamination before it starts. Follow this checklist once and you will see why our crews finish every kitchen in a fraction of the time most people expect.
