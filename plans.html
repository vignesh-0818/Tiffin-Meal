Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our plans

Each daily meal includes a balanced combination of roti/rice, sabzi, dal, salad, and a dessert on select days. The exact menu rotates weekly to keep things fresh and exciting. All meals are freshly prepared and delivered to your doorstep.

Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Changes will take effect from the next billing cycle. If you upgrade mid-month, you'll only be charged the prorated difference. Contact our support team to make any changes.

You can cancel your subscription anytime with a 7-day notice. There are no cancellation fees. If you cancel mid-cycle, you'll continue to receive meals until the end of your current billing period. Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days.

We deliver meals twice daily — lunch between 12:00 PM and 1:30 PM and dinner between 7:00 PM and 8:30 PM. Combo plan subscribers enjoy priority delivery with earlier time slots. You can set your preferred delivery window in the customer dashboard.

Absolutely. During registration, you can specify allergies and dietary preferences such as no onions, no garlic, gluten-free, or Jain options. Our kitchen is equipped to handle a wide range of dietary needs. Our nutritionist can also help customize your meals.

We accept all major credit/debit cards, UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), net banking, and wallet payments. Monthly auto-renewal is available for hassle-free payments. You can also opt for quarterly or annual plans for additional savings.
