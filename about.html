About

Our Story

TiffinBox was born in 2019 from a simple observation: thousands of working professionals, students, and families in Delhi were struggling to find affordable, nutritious, and home-style meals every single day.

It all started in a small kitchen in Lajpat Nagar with just 5 tiffins a day. Our founder, Arjun Mehta, noticed his colleagues skipping meals or relying on unhealthy fast food because they had no time to cook and no one to turn to for simple, wholesome food.

Drawing inspiration from his mother's kitchen, Arjun began preparing homestyle meals with fresh ingredients and delivering them personally on his scooter. Word spread quickly. Within three months, we were serving 50 customers daily.

Today, TiffinBox operates multiple kitchens across Delhi NCR, serving over 2,000 satisfied customers every day. But our promise remains the same: every meal should taste like it was made by someone who cares.

Our Mission

We believe that no one should have to choose between a busy schedule and a healthy meal. Our mission is to make nutritious, home-style food accessible to everyone at an affordable price.

We work directly with local farmers and vendors to source the freshest ingredients. Our kitchens follow strict hygiene protocols, and our menu is crafted by experienced home chefs who understand the importance of balanced nutrition.

From sourcing to delivery, every step in our process is designed with care. We use eco-friendly packaging, maintain temperature-controlled delivery, and ensure every tiffin reaches you within 30 minutes of preparation.

"Nourishing lives, one tiffin at a time."
